Privacy policy隱私權政策

Applies to the Memfolio browser extension. In force since 2026-10-08.適用於 Memfolio 瀏覽器擴充功能。自 2026-10-08 起生效。

In short摘要

Memfolio saves photos and videos from the profile pages you open on instagram.com into folders on your own computer. It collects no personal data, sends nothing to us or to anyone else, has no server of its own, and contains no analytics, error reporting or advertising. Memfolio 把你在 instagram.com 開啟的個人檔案頁上的相片與影片存進你自己電腦的資料夾。它不收集個人資料，不把任何東西送給我們或其他人，沒有自己的伺服器，也不含分析、錯誤回報或廣告。

What is stored on your computer存在你電腦上的資料

The extension keeps the following in the browser's extension storage, on your device only:擴充功能只在你的裝置上、瀏覽器的擴充功能儲存空間中保存以下內容：

  • Your settings: interface language, time format, where single downloads go, the name of the subfolder, and the state of a few switches.你的設定：介面語言、時間格式、單項下載的存放位置、子資料夾名稱，以及幾個開關的狀態。
  • The account list: for each account you download, its username, its numeric id on the platform, the time of the last run, the file count seen then, and the groups and order you arranged.帳號清單：每個你下載過的帳號，其帳號名稱、平台上的數字 id、上次執行時間、當時看到的檔案數，以及你安排的群組與順序。
  • In Chrome and Edge: a reference to the folder you chose for each account (a File System Access handle). In Firefox: the folder's path inside the browser's download folder.在 Chrome 與 Edge：你為每個帳號選擇的資料夾的參照（File System Access handle）。在 Firefox：該資料夾在瀏覽器下載資料夾內的路徑。

The downloaded files themselves are written to the folders you chose and are not read back for any purpose other than telling which files are already there.下載的檔案本身寫入你選擇的資料夾；除了判斷哪些檔案已經存在之外，不會再被讀取。

What is sent over the network經由網路傳送的內容

The extension talks to instagram.com only, from the page you have open and with the session you are already logged in with, to list the posts of that profile and to fetch the media files. These are the same requests the site makes when you scroll and open posts. No request goes to any other host, and nothing about you, your account or your downloads is transmitted to us. 擴充功能只與 instagram.com 連線，從你開啟的頁面、以你已登入的 session，列出該個人檔案的貼文並取得媒體檔案。這些請求與你捲動頁面、開啟貼文時網站自己發出的請求相同。不會連到任何其他主機，也不會把關於你、你的帳號或你的下載的任何資訊傳給我們。

The extension loads no remote code and contains no third-party libraries that phone home.擴充功能不載入遠端程式碼，也不含任何會回傳資料的第三方程式庫。

Permissions權限

  • storage: the settings and the account list described above.storage：上述的設定與帳號清單。
  • Access to www.instagram.com: the extension runs on that site only, to add its buttons to profile and post pages and to read the media information those pages already loaded.存取 www.instagram.com：擴充功能只在該網站執行，在個人檔案與貼文頁加入按鈕，並讀取頁面已載入的媒體資訊。
  • Firefox only, downloads: to write files into the browser's download folder, since Firefox gives extensions no other access to folders.僅 Firefox，downloads：把檔案寫入瀏覽器的下載資料夾，因為 Firefox 不開放擴充功能以其他方式存取資料夾。

Use of data from the site網站資料的使用

The extension reads information from instagram.com for one purpose: saving the media of the profile you are viewing into your folders and skipping files you already have. It does not use that information for anything else, does not build profiles, does not sell, share or transfer it, and does not use it for advertising or credit purposes. Which media you can see is decided by the site and your account; the extension does not bypass logins, privacy settings or paywalls. 擴充功能從 instagram.com 讀取資訊只有一個目的：把你正在瀏覽的個人檔案的媒體存進你的資料夾，並略過你已經有的檔案。它不會把這些資訊用於其他用途，不建立個人輪廓，不出售、分享或轉移，也不用於廣告或信用評估。你能看到哪些媒體由網站與你的帳號決定；擴充功能不繞過登入、隱私設定或付費牆。

Deleting your data刪除資料

Remove an account from the list to delete its entry. Removing the extension deletes all of its stored settings and the account list. The downloaded files stay in your folders until you delete them yourself. 從清單移除帳號即刪除該筆資料。移除擴充功能會刪除所有儲存的設定與帳號清單。下載的檔案會留在你的資料夾，直到你自行刪除。

Changes and contact變更與聯絡

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. Questions go to the project's issue tracker on GitHub. 本政策的變更會在本頁發布並更新日期。問題請到專案的 GitHub issue。
